About Me

I am a simulation engineer working on functional instruction validation of the A RISC-V Multithreaded Core Instruction Set. My role involves writing assembly and C-based instruction tests, executing them on the RTL model, comparing register dumps, and validating with spike/QEMU outputs. My recent work focused on validating memory, shift, and branch instructions using C.
